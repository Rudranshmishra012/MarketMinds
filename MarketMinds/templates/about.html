About MarketMinds

Smarter Markets. Better Decisions for Farmers.

MarketMinds is a decision-support prototype built to help farmers move beyond "sell at the nearest mandi" and instead make a calculated choice: which market, which buyer, and which day actually leaves the most money in their pocket once transport, storage, and other costs are accounted for.

Every recommendation on this platform is generated by backend calculations — expected net profit, a sell-now-vs-wait comparison, and a transparent buyer match score — never a random or canned suggestion.
